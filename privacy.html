FishFish Privacy

Last updated October 4, 2026

FishFish is a private messenger for two people. It has no accounts, servers, ads or analytics of its own.

Where your messages live

Messages, photos, voice notes, hearts and to-dos are stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) and shared only with the one person you invite. They stay in the inviting person's iCloud storage. The developer can't read them.

On your phone

FishFish keeps a copy of your conversation, photos and voice notes on your device so it works offline. Photos are re-saved without location or camera details. Files are stored with iOS data protection.

Camera, microphone and notifications

The camera and microphone are used only when you take a photo or record a voice note to send. Notifications are optional and only say that something new arrived, or that a reminder is due.

Deleting

You can delete any message, and it is removed for both people. Stopping sharing or deleting the app removes the conversation from that device. iCloud data can also be managed in iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud.

Testing

If you use FishFish through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer, as described in TestFlight's own terms.
